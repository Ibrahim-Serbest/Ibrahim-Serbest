## Reporting & Quality Evidence

Reporting is designed around the needs of the business, delivery teams, and the specific project—not limited to a single fixed output. The automation frameworks I work with capture failures and can provide configurable evidence such as:

- Failure screenshots for faster diagnosis
- Before-and-after screenshots when the workflow requires comparison
- Full-page screenshots when complete page context is important
- Screenshots from specific pages or checkpoints requested by business stakeholders
- Cucumber and xUnit test results
- HTML and PDF reporting outputs
- Execution evidence that supports defect investigation, release readiness, and deployment decisions

The goal is clear, useful, traceable reporting that helps both technical teams and business stakeholders understand what ran, what passed, what failed, and what evidence supports the result.
